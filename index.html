Server Rules

Short version: don't ruin it for everyone else. Full list below.

01

Respect

  1. No meanness, harassment, hate speech, or discrimination. Be decent to other players.
  2. English is the primary language in global chat, for moderation purposes.
  3. No spamming, chat flooding, or excessive caps.
02

No claims, no excuses

  1. This server has no land claim or chunk protection plugin — everything is built on trust.
  2. No stealing from chests, farms, or storage that isn't yours.
  3. No griefing: don't break, burn, flood, or edit builds that aren't yours.
  4. Traps designed purely to steal, grief, or ruin someone's base are not allowed.
03

Economy

  1. Duplication glitches must be reported, not used. Exploiting them is a permanent ban.
  2. Real-money trading for in-game currency or items is not allowed.
  3. Shops must list prices clearly. Scamming other players results in an economy ban.
04

Fair play

  1. No cheat clients, X-ray, or unfair advantage mods.
  2. Advertising other servers in chat is not allowed.
  3. Staff decisions are final. Appeals go through the Discord ticket system.

Wiki / Fishing

Fishing

Fish can be caught with a fishing rod or a crab trap in any water or lava source, whether it was placed by a player or generated naturally. What bites depends on where your bobber sits and the conditions at that moment.

Where

Biome & water type

Ocean, river and freshwater each have their own pool. In the Nether the fish depend on the biome.

When

Time of day

Fish are marked day, night or any time, so some only bite after dark.

Season

Spring to winter

The pool changes every season. Many fish only appear in one or two.

Weather

Clear or rain

Some fish need clear skies and others only show up in rain or snow.

Wiki / Skills / Overview

Skills

Skills are activities you level up through play. Each skill has 12 levels. Every level fills an XP bar and awards a point to spend in the Skills Panel (K by default). At levels 5, 8 and 12 your path splits in two. The path you skip is locked unless you use a Crystal of Regret to reset. Every skill has at least one level 12 node that gives a way to get Prismatic Shards.

Wiki / Farming / Basics

Farming

Crops come from seeds and fruit trees and are used for money, cooking, quests and artisan goods. Farming is simple once you know the daily rhythm.

Till

Use a hoe on dirt to make farmland, then right-click seeds onto it. Planted crops do not drop seeds when broken.

Hydrate

Nearby water does not hydrate farmland. Use watering cans, sprinklers, rain, water bottles or splash potions. Dry farmland can turn to dirt and destroy the crop.

Seasons

Most crops only grow in certain seasons. Wrong-season crops stay planted but do not grow. Some biomes, like Taiga, are a permanent season.

Fertilize

Right-click farmland with fertilizer for faster growth, better quality, hydration or more yield. A pitchfork has a 50% chance to recover it.

Growth

At 6:00 AM, watered crops in season grow one stage. At 100% right-click to harvest. Diamond, Netherite and Neptunium hoes harvest several at once.

Quality

Normal, Silver, Gold or Iridium. Better fertilizer and seeds from the Seed Maker raise quality. Iridium needs Pristine Quality Fertilizer.

Sprinklers can sit above or level with farmland, so staggered farms work.

Wiki / Farming / Fruit Trees

Fruit Trees

Fruits can be harvested from fruit trees and animals. They are mainly used for money, cooking, quests and artisan goods. Plant a sapling, wait for the right season and come back to pick.

Plant

Saplings

Right-click a dirt block with a sapling to plant the matching tree.

Grow

Tree growth

Trees only grow during their own season, and the time it takes is random. Tree Fertilizer speeds them up.

Harvest

Picking fruit

Right-click the fruit to harvest it, or let a Drum of the Cornucopia do it automatically.

Season

Harvest windows

Fruit can only be picked in its season, and the time it takes to ripen is random too.

Tips

  • Two kinds of sapling. Apples and cherries come as a plain sapling (seasonal, bigger yield) and as a tree sapling (dark cherry for cherries). The tree versions keep growing year-round but yield less per harvest.
  • Skill perks. Each fruit harvest gives 30 Farming XP, and Tree Whisperer (Farming level 8) adds +2 fruit when you harvest by hand. Extra fruit gives no bonus XP.
  • Banana Karenina. This skill book doubles Banana Tree output. It has a 0.1% chance to drop from a Banana Tree.
  • Prismatic Shard saplings. Lemon, Hazenut, Pawpaw and Cinnamon saplings can be bought with prismatic shards from the Baker, so save your shards from level 12 skill nodes.

Wiki / Reference / Skill Books

Skill Books

Skill Books give you special abilities when read. Most are rare drops tied to the task they help with, so the best way to find one is to keep doing that task.

How to get them

  • Do the task the book is about and wait for the drop chance (see the Source column).
  • Buy from the Wandering Trader, who rolls 2 books out of all possible books each time they spawn.
  • Buy from the Bookseller, who appears on the phone in the Late Season.
  • Each season has 2 unique books for sale that can only be bought in that season.
  • Win one from the Prize Machine.

Wiki / Slimes / Care

Feeding, Largos and Happiness

Each breed has a specific diet, shown in the Jade panel when you hover over it. Slimes eat a dropped item, a living mob, or food from a Slime Feeder.

Slime categories

Pure

Pure Slimes

The basic slime from a Slime Incubator, with only one breed. Max size 3. Feed one a Plort of another breed and it becomes a Largo.

Largo

Largo Slimes

Made when a Pure Slime eats another breed's Plort. Max size 4. They inherit the diet, favorite food and Plorts of both breeds, so they are the best for farming.

Danger

Tarr

A Largo that eats a third kind of Plort becomes a Tarr. Tarrs eat Largos, Pure Slimes and players, grow after each meal and turn eaten slimes into Tarrs. Keep your pen tidy.

When a slime eats

  • Below its max size (3 for Pure slimes), the slime grows.
  • It is healed by 1 heart.
  • At size 3 or more, it produces a Plort of its breed. A Largo also produces a Plort of its sub-breed.
  • It gets happier, and its happiness stops dropping if it was starving.
  • If it eats its favorite food (as an item, a mob, or both), it produces an additional Plort for each breed.

Experiment with a slime's diet and work out its favorite foods.

Happiness

Every slime has a happiness value that rises or falls with what happens to it. A newly spawned slime starts at half of the maximum. The happiness system is still a work in progress and will be expanded.

Goes up when

  • It feeds. Its favorite food doubles the increase.
  • It feeds near at least 3 slimes of the same breed (Largos count) for a small boost, or near 6 to 7 for a bigger one.
  • It eats Slime Candy, which moves it up to the next happiness stage (for example sad to neutral).

Goes down when

  • It is starving, which lowers happiness gradually.
  • It is killed, which greatly lowers happiness.
  • It feeds while crowded (near 9 or more slimes of the same breed), which greatly lowers happiness.

Happiness states

Best

Happy

Performs its positive behaviors, more often the happier it is.

Default

Neutral

No negative behaviors and no positive ones. Incubated slimes are born in this state.

Low

Sad

Still produces Plorts, but performs negative behaviors more and more as happiness drops.

Danger

Furious

Attacks the player, often performs negative behaviors, makes no Plorts and will not turn into a Largo.

Wiki / Getting Started / Getting Started

Getting Started

The first thing to do after spawning is forage for sellable goods and find a good spot for a home. Everything below is a quick path through your first days.

Sell

Shipping Bin

Anything with a coin amount in its tooltip is sellable. Put it in a Shipping Bin and it sells at 6am every morning.

Forage

Early money

Pumpkins, berry bushes and bales of crops give a good starting cash boost. Chests hold food, experience books and equipment.

Quests

Quest book

Follow the Getting Started quests to learn tool and weapon upgrades. They also award a Farm House you can place without building.

Settle

Pick a biome

Beach and savanna biomes are permanent summer, which makes them a good place to start a farm.

Farming

Water

Every morning

Water crops with a Watering Can after 6am. Dry farmland can turn to dirt and break the crop. Sprinklers hydrate nearby farmland at 6am instead.

Harvest

Right-click

Harvesting keeps the seed in the ground. A seed pouch plants a 3x3 when shifting. Diamond hoes and above harvest 5x5, lower hoes 3x3.

Process

Add value

Processing raises value. Two tomatoes make tomato sauce in the cooking pot. Pumpkin bread, dumplings and jams are good early recipes.

Mining and the Nether

Overworld

Ores and geodes

Find vanilla ores and zinc. Earth crystals are used in many artisan machine recipes, the cooking pot and the cutting board. Geodes can drop aquamarine, which makes crab traps.

Crab traps

Rare goodies

Crab traps fish up items like slime hearts and neptunium nuggets.

Nether

Flint and steel

Buy flint and steel from the Blacksmith, since it cannot be crafted. The Nether has raw bismuth ore, fire crystals, bastions and fortresses. Bastions can hold coins worth 32K.

Batteries

Charging Rods

Blaze rods make Charging Rods. When struck by lightning they drop a battery after 4 days. Batteries power advanced and automated machines.

Skills. Doing activities earns skill XP. Each level gives a point to spend with K by default. Higher levels change how you farm, so plan around them. Everything can be automated.

Longwings

Longwings is the modpack term for butterflies and moths. They are Adventurer Products and can be sold, with a price based on rarity and size.

Price (16 - rarity) x 78 + size bonusSize bonus Size / 0.01 x 4Sizes Small 0.75, Normal 0.95, Larger 1.0
Metamorphosize book. This skill book triples the size bonus. At size 1.0 it adds 800 to the price.

Drops and breeding

Amber and pollen

Every 5 minutes

Butterflies drop Butterfly Amber and moths drop Moth Pollen. Chance = unique nearby flowers x 0.15 minus nearby longwings x 0.1, capped at 100%.

Eggs

A quarter of the chance

Caterpillar Eggs use the same rules at a quarter of the chance. Parents are the nearest longwings when the egg spawns. Hatch eggs in a Caterpillar Box or sell them.

Species

Cross-breeding

Both parents decide the species, so cross-breeding can reveal new species without lures.

Size

Average of parents

Egg size is the average of both parents. The hatchling keeps it if it is under double the breed max size.

Getting rarer longwings

Offspring can only be one tier rarer than the rarest parent (lower number is rarer). 1) Breed two tier 5s to get a tier 4. 2) Breed that tier 4 with any longwing to get a tier 3. 3) Always keep your rarest longwing as a parent.
